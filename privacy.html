TimeShift Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026. Applies to TimeShift 0.1.2 and later.

TimeShift converts time expressions you type or paste into its panel to your device's local timezone. Conversion runs entirely within the extension on your device.

Local processing

When you choose Convert, TimeShift processes the text in the panel, your device's timezone, browser language, and current date and time locally. Neither typing nor converting sends this information to the developer or any external service. Conversion works without an internet connection and does not use AI, Render, or OpenAI.

TimeShift does not automatically read webpage contents, browsing history, cookies, or other tabs. You choose the text by typing or pasting it into the panel. It does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and does not collect, sell, or share your conversion data.

Storage and retention

The extension saves the panel's position in Chrome local storage. It does not save input text or conversion history to persistent extension storage. Closing the panel removes that panel's input and results. Removing the extension clears its extension storage.

The developer does not receive or retain conversion text or results. No TimeShift account or API key is required.

Permissions and external links

activeTab and scripting let TimeShift open its floating panel in the page you choose when you click the toolbar icon. storage remembers the panel position. The extension requests no API host permissions. Its packaged code performs conversion locally and does not load remotely hosted executable code.

The included privacy page is available offline. If you choose to open the public support page or contact the developer by email, that external website or email provider handles your visit or message under its own policies. Avoid including private text or credentials in a public support issue.

Contact and changes

Contact the developer at yitao77v@gmail.com or through the TimeShift support page or the Chrome Web Store support contact. You can request information about this policy or deletion of information you have sent directly to the developer through these channels.

Updates to this policy will be published in this repository and included in future extension releases.
